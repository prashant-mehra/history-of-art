Bust of Nefertiti

Thutmose · c. 1345 BCE

ArtistThutmose
Datec. 1345 BCE
TypeSculpture
MediumPainted limestone and stucco
LocationNeues Museum, Berlin
RegionAfrica
EraAncient Egypt

Found in 1912 in the workshop of the sculptor Thutmose at Amarna. The queen's elegant long neck and balanced features have made it one of the best known faces of the ancient world. Egypt has asked for its return.
