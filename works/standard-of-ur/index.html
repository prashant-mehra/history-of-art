Standard of Ur

Unknown artist · c. 2600–2400 BCE

Datec. 2600–2400 BCE
TypeCraft & Decorative
MediumShell, lapis lazuli and red limestone on bitumen
LocationBritish Museum, London
RegionAsia
EraAncient Near East

A hollow box decorated with mosaic scenes, found in a royal grave at Ur (modern Iraq). One side shows war, the other a banquet, read as registers from bottom to top. Its original purpose is unknown.
