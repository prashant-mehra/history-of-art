Terracotta Army

Unknown artist · c. 210 BCE

Datec. 210 BCE
TypeSculpture
MediumTerracotta
LocationXi'an, China
RegionAsia

Thousands of life-size clay soldiers buried near the tomb of Qin Shi Huang, the first emperor of China. Each face differs. Discovered by farmers in 1974.
