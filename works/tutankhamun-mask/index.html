Mask of Tutankhamun

Unknown artist · c. 1323 BCE

Datec. 1323 BCE
TypeCraft & Decorative
MediumGold inlaid with lapis lazuli, glass and quartz
LocationEgyptian Museum, Cairo
RegionAfrica
EraAncient Egypt

The funerary mask lay over the mummy of the young pharaoh in his tomb, found by Howard Carter in 1922. It weighs roughly 10 kg and shows the king with the royal headcloth and false beard.
