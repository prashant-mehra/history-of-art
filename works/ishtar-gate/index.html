Ishtar Gate

Unknown artist · c. 575 BCE

Datec. 575 BCE
TypeArchitecture
MediumGlazed brick
LocationPergamon Museum, Berlin (reconstruction)
RegionAsia
EraAncient Near East

The eighth gate of inner Babylon, built under Nebuchadnezzar II and faced with blue glazed bricks bearing bulls and dragons. German archaeologists excavated it and rebuilt it in Berlin.
