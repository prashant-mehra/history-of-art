Unknown artist · c. 800

Datec. 800
TypeManuscript & Textile
MediumInk and pigment on vellum
LocationTrinity College, Dublin
RegionEurope

An illuminated manuscript of the four Gospels made by monks, covered in interlace, animals and Celtic spirals. The pages for key moments, like the Chi Rho page, are astonishingly detailed.
