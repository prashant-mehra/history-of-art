Stele of Hammurabi

Unknown artist · c. 1754 BCE

Datec. 1754 BCE
TypeSculpture
MediumBasalt
LocationLouvre, Paris
RegionAsia
EraAncient Near East

A tall stone pillar inscribed in cuneiform with nearly 300 laws. At the top the Babylonian king Hammurabi stands before a seated god, shown receiving the authority to rule. It was found at Susa in 1901.
