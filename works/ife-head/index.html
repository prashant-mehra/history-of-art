Ife head

Unknown artist · 12th–15th century

Date12th–15th century
TypeSculpture
MediumCopper alloy
LocationIfe, Nigeria
RegionAfrica

Naturalistic heads cast by the Yoruba people at Ife, striking to European visitors who could not believe African origins. Fine lines on the face may represent scarification or a veil.

Bronze and casting
