About

This site follows the story of art chronologically, from the Stone Age to the present, with regional, thematic and technical pages alongside. Most pages are generated from data files in tools/ by tools/build.py.

Accuracy

Dates for prehistoric art are estimates and are frequently revised. Where sources disagree, the pages say "about" or give a range. Interpretations of meaning are hypotheses, not facts.

Images

Photographs and reproductions come from Wikimedia Commons and are either in the public domain or under an open license that requires credit. Each image is credited beneath it, and every credit is listed on the image credits page. Works still under copyright, such as most art made after about 1930, have no image here.
