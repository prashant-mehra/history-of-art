Pigments and paint

Pigments are colored powders mixed with a binder. Early artists used ochre, charcoal and manganese. Later came lapis lazuli blue, vermilion, lead white and, in the 19th century, synthetic colors. The choice of pigment and binder (egg, oil, acrylic) shapes a painting's look.
