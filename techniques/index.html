Bronze and casting

Metal poured into a mold. Lost-wax casting, used from ancient Greece to Ife and Benin, allows very fine detail.

Carving in stone

Sculptors remove material, revealing a form. Marble is prized for its translucence. Michelangelo spoke of freeing a figure from the block.

Printmaking

Images printed from a prepared surface: woodcut, engraving, etching, lithography. Multiples let art circulate widely and cheaply.
