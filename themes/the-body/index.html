The human body

From fertility figures to athletes and self-portraits, artists keep returning to the body.

The Venus figurines may celebrate fertility. Greek sculptors idealized the athletic male nude. Michelangelo's David presents ideal strength.

Modern and contemporary artists question whose bodies are shown and how: Manet's Olympia stares back; Kahlo paints her own wounds.

Bourgeois and Kusama explore the body as memory and obsession.
