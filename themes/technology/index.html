Technology and change

New tools change what art looks like: oil paint, the printing press, photography, and now software.

Concrete let Romans build the Pantheon dome. Printing made Dürer's images widely available. The Eiffel Tower celebrated iron.

Photography pushed painters toward Impressionism and abstraction. Warhol used silkscreen to mimic mass production.

Digital artists like Beeple work with code, screens and networks.
