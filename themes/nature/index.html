Nature and landscape

Nature has been a subject, a source of materials and a mirror of feeling.

Ice Age painters drew the animals they depended on. Chinese landscape painters saw mountains as images of cosmic order.

Romantic painters made nature sublime, Impressionists studied its changing light, and modern architects such as Wright tried to merge with it.

Today, climate change is a major theme.
