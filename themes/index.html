Themes

Cross-era essays that follow one idea through art history.

Religion and the sacred

Much of the world's art was made to honor gods or to guide worship.

Power and politics

Rulers have used art to proclaim authority, and artists have used it to question authority.

Nature and landscape

Nature has been a subject, a source of materials and a mirror of feeling.

The human body

From fertility figures to athletes and self-portraits, artists keep returning to the body.

Technology and change

New tools change what art looks like: oil paint, the printing press, photography, and now software.
