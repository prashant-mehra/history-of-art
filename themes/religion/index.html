Religion and the sacred

Much of the world's art was made to honor gods or to guide worship.

Egyptian tomb painting prepared the dead for the afterlife. Greek temples housed images of gods. Gothic cathedrals aimed to bring heaven to earth with light.

Different faiths solve the problem of picturing the divine differently: icons in Orthodox Christianity, geometric pattern and calligraphy in Islam, mandalas in Buddhism.

Even after art left the church, it kept sacred themes, from Caravaggio's saints to Rothko's chapel.
