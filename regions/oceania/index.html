Oceania

Oceania includes Australia, Melanesia, Micronesia and Polynesia, with art ranging from rock art tens of thousands of years old to monumental carving.

Australia

Aboriginal rock art is among the oldest continuous art traditions on Earth. The Papunya Tula movement (from 1971) brought Western Desert painting to world attention.

Polynesia

Moai on Rapa Nui, Māori carving (whakairo) and tattoo (tā moko), and Hawaiian featherwork.

Melanesia

Sepik River carvings, Asmat shields and elaborate masks of Papua New Guinea and Vanuatu.

Navigation and art

Stick charts from the Marshall Islands show swells and islands, an art of wayfinding.
