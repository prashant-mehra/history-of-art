Africa

Africa is home to some of the oldest art in the world, from Blombos Cave engravings in South Africa to the Sahara rock art of Tassili n'Ajjer. It includes Egypt and Nubia in the north, as well as the many sculpture traditions of West, Central and Southern Africa.

Ancient Egypt and Nubia

Egypt's monuments dominate public imagination, but Nubian kingdoms such as Kush also built pyramids and temples.

West African sculpture

The terracotta Nok figures (~1000 BCE–300 CE), the bronzes of Ife and Benin, and later masks of many peoples show a wide range of styles.

Great Zimbabwe

Dry-stone walls built between about the 11th and 15th centuries.

Textiles and modern art

Kente cloth, bogolanfini and Ethiopian illuminated manuscripts sit alongside modern artists like El Anatsui and Sokari Douglas Camp.

Restitution

Many works were taken during colonial rule; museums and governments are negotiating returns.
