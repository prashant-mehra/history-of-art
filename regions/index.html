Regions

Art traditions that run alongside the main timeline, from Africa, Asia, the Americas and Oceania.

Africa

Africa is home to some of the oldest art in the world, from Blombos Cave engravings in South Africa to the Sahara rock art of Tassili n'Ajjer. It includes Egypt and Nubia in the north, as well as the many sculpture traditions of West, Central and Southern Africa.

Asia

Asia spans several of the world's longest continuous art traditions.

The Americas

Indigenous peoples of the Americas developed monumental architecture, textiles and metalwork long before European contact, and their traditions continue today.

Oceania

Oceania includes Australia, Melanesia, Micronesia and Polynesia, with art ranging from rock art tens of thousands of years old to monumental carving.
