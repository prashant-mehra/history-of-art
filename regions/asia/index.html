Asia

Asia spans several of the world's longest continuous art traditions.

China

Bronzes of the Shang dynasty, the Terracotta Army, ink landscape painting, calligraphy and porcelain. Landscape painting reached a peak in the Song dynasty (960–1279).

Japan

From Jōmon pottery to Buddhist sculpture, Zen ink painting, ukiyo-e prints and contemporary art by Kusama and Murakami.

India and South Asia

Rock-cut temples at Ajanta and Ellora, Chola bronzes, Mughal miniature painting and the Taj Mahal.

Southeast Asia

Borobudur in Java and Angkor Wat in Cambodia are the greatest temple complexes.

West and Central Asia

Persian miniatures, Islamic architecture and the ancient Near East. Silk Road cities linked these traditions to China.
